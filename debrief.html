🌊 Flood Rule #1

Always move to HIGH GROUND immediately. Never wait. Water rises faster than you think — a 6-inch current can knock you down.

🌉 Bridge Rule

Never cross a bridge during flooding. Even 2 inches of fast-moving water can sweep a vehicle off a bridge. Turn around, don't drown.

🏚️ Earthquake Rule #1

DROP — Cover — Hold On. Get under a sturdy table. Stay away from windows. Do not run outside during shaking.

🔥 Fire Evacuation

Stay LOW under smoke. Feel doors before opening. Never use elevators. Meet at a pre-planned spot outside.

⏱ The 90-Second Rule

Research shows the first 90 seconds determine survival in most disasters. Train your instincts NOW — not when it happens.
